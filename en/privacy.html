Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Who we are

Marco Zero Labs is a software studio in Recife, Pernambuco, Brazil, and is responsible for this site.

What data this site collects

This site uses no cookies, has no forms and does not ask you to sign up.

  • Metrics: if we turn on audience metrics, they will be anonymous and aggregated, with no cookies. They are not active today.
  • Preferences: the theme and device you choose stay in your browser and are not sent to us.
  • Device system: the site identifies the system inside your browser to choose the frame around the images. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Calculator: the math runs in your browser, and the numbers are not sent to us.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical access data, such as your IP address, for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Contact: if you write to us, we use what you send only to reply.

This policy covers this site only. The Ritmo app and portal will have their own terms.

Your rights

Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018, art. 18) gives you, among other rights, the right to:

  • confirm whether we process your data and access it;
  • correct incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  • ask for unnecessary or unlawfully processed data to be anonymized, blocked or deleted;
  • ask for data portability;
  • know who we share your data with;
  • withdraw consent you gave.

To use these rights, write to the address above. You can also contact Brazil’s National Data Protection Authority.
