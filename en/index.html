Why trust it

These are project requirements, tested as the product is built. Ritmo does not hold any certifications yet.

  • The figure does not come from AI

    Artificial intelligence understands the question and writes the sentence. Ritmo gets the figure straight from your company’s system (the ERP) and shows where it came from and when it was updated.

  • Read-only in the first version

    Ritmo reads data. It does not create, change or delete anything in your company’s system.

  • Everyone sees only what is theirs

    Each company sees only its own data, and each seller sees only their own portfolio.

  • Sign in without a password

    Sign in with your fingerprint, your face or a code sent to your phone. There is no password to leak or forget.

  • Protected data

    Your data travels and stays stored protected (encrypted).

  • LGPD, Brazil’s data protection law

    Your company decides about the data, and we sign a data protection agreement before receiving any real information. Part of the service uses companies outside Brazil, listed in that agreement.

  • No route tracking

    Location is used only while the route is open. Ritmo records the visit, with the customer and the time, but not the path travelled.

Frequently asked questions

Didn’t find your question? Talk to us.

Do I need to change systems?

No. Ritmo reads data from the system your company already uses and changes nothing in it.

Where do the figures come from?

From your company’s system. Artificial intelligence only understands the question and writes the sentence, and every answer shows where the figure came from and when it was updated.

Does Ritmo track the seller?

It does not record the path travelled. Location is used only while the route is open, and Ritmo records the visit, with the customer and the time.

Is it safe?

Security is a project requirement: each company sees only its own data, each seller only their own portfolio, with no passwords and with encrypted data. We sign a data protection agreement before receiving any real data. Ritmo is still under construction and holds no certifications.
