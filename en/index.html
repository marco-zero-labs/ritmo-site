The problem

The information exists, but it does not reach the people on the street.

  • Before the visit

    You arrive without knowing the last order, what the customer stopped buying or whether an invoice is overdue.

  • On the route

    A simple lookup turns into a call or a voice message to the office, and the answer takes time.

  • At the end of the month

    Managers see the result when it is too late to act, and customers stop buying without anyone noticing.

How it works

The product is under construction. This is how it is designed to work.

Ask

Write the way you would to a colleague. The answer gives the figure, where it came from and when the data was updated.

Route of the day

Your visits in the best order, calculated by Ritmo, and one tap to open the next stop in Google Maps or Waze.

Alerts

A notice when a customer falls out of their buying rhythm, drops in volume or has an overdue invoice, on WhatsApp or your phone.

On every screen

Phone, tablet, laptop and WhatsApp, with the same figures on all of them.

Illustrative screens with fictional companies and figures. The product is still under construction, and the final result may change.

Why trust it

A wrong figure is costly in front of a customer. That is why Ritmo is designed this way:

  • The figure comes from the ERP, not the AI

    Artificial intelligence understands the question and writes the sentence. Figures come from fixed queries on your company's data, and every answer shows its source and last update.

  • Read-only in the MVP

    Ritmo reads data. It does not create, change or delete anything in your company's system.

Security and privacy

These are project requirements, tested as the product is built. Ritmo does not hold any certifications yet.

  • Isolation per customer

    Each company sees only its own data, and each seller sees only their own portfolio.

  • Sign-in without passwords

    Sign in with a one-time code or a passkey. There is no password to leak or forget.

  • Encryption

    Data is protected in transit and at rest.

  • LGPD, Brazil's data protection law

    Your company decides about the data, and we sign a data protection agreement before receiving any real information. Part of the service uses providers outside Brazil, listed in that agreement.

  • No route tracking

    Location is used only while the route is open. Ritmo does not record the path a seller travels.
