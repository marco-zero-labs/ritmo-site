Ritmo · The sales copilot for people who sell on the street

Walk into every visit knowing what to sell. Ritmo brings your company’s data together and answers instantly on WhatsApp, the app or your computer, with alerts and the route of the day.

Become a pilot company See how it works

The information exists, but it does not reach the people on the street

  • Before the visit

    You arrive without knowing the last order, what the customer stopped buying or whether an invoice is overdue.

  • On the route

    A simple question turns into a call or a voice message to the office, and the answer takes time.

  • At the end of the month

    Managers see the result when it is too late to act, and some customers stop buying without anyone noticing.

Who it is for

  • Manufacturers

    That sell through sales reps and need the information to reach them.

  • Distributors

    With teams on the street and a large portfolio of customers to visit.

  • Independent sales reps

    Who serve many customers on their own and need information right away, without calling the office.

  • Field sellers and managers

    Who follow the target and customer coverage and want to act before the end of the month.

Why trust it

  • The figure comes from the ERP, not the AI

    Artificial intelligence understands the question and writes the sentence. Ritmo gets the figure straight from your company’s system and shows where it came from and when it was updated.

  • Read-only in the first version

    Ritmo reads data. It does not create, change or delete anything in your company’s system.

  • Everyone sees only what is theirs

    Each company sees only its own data, and each seller sees only their own portfolio.

Security and privacy

These are project requirements, tested as the product is built. Ritmo does not hold any certifications yet.

  • Isolation per company

    One company’s data does not mix with another’s.

  • Sign in without a password

    Sign in with your fingerprint, your face or a code sent to your phone. There is no password to leak or forget.

  • Protected data

    Your data travels and stays stored protected (encrypted).

  • LGPD, Brazil’s data protection law

    Your company decides about the data, and we sign a data protection agreement before receiving any real information. Part of the service uses companies outside Brazil, listed in that agreement.

  • No route tracking

    Location is used only while the route is open. Ritmo records the visit, with the customer and the time, but not the path travelled.

Frequently asked questions

Did not find your question? Talk to us.

Does it work with my ERP?

We are starting with the ERPs of the pilot companies. We also accept spreadsheets.

How much does it cost?

The price is being set together with the pilot companies.

Do I need to install anything?

WhatsApp already works. The app is optional.

Is my data safe?

Security is a project requirement: each company sees only its own data, each seller only their own portfolio, with no passwords and with encrypted data. We sign a data protection agreement before receiving any real data. Ritmo is still under construction and holds no certifications.

Does it work offline?

The first version needs a signal. An offline mode is on the plan.

Do I need to change systems?

No. Ritmo reads data from the system your company already uses and changes nothing in it.
